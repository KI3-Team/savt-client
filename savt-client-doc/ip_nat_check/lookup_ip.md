# Welcome

Dear worker! We should check your IP before we start the test. Please follow the instructions based on your operating system to find your **local IPv4 address**:

## Windows 11
1. Open the Windows **Start** menu and click **Settings**.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_11.png =750x)

2. Click **Network & internet** in the left panel and then click **Properties**.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_12.png =750x)

3. Here, you can see your local **IPv4 address**.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_13.png =750x)

## Windows 10
1. Open the Windows **Start** menu and click **Settings**.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_21.png =350x)

2. Select **Network & Internet**.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_22.png =750x)

3. Select **Wi-Fi** in the left panel and then click **Advanced Settings**. (For wired connections, select **Ethernet** and then click **Network connection**.) Your local IP will appear in the next window.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_23.png =750x)

4. View your local IP address under **IPv4 address**.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_24.png =750x)

## MacOS
1. Open the **Apple menu** and click **System Settings**.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_31.png =350x)

2. Click **Network** in the left panel and then select **Wi-Fi** or **Ethernet** (for wired connections).

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_32.png =750x)

3. Click **Details** next to the network you’re connected to.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_33.png =750x)

4. Scroll down to see your Mac’s local **IP address**.

    ![Alt](/public/sav/ip_nat_check/ip_nat_check_34.png =750x)
